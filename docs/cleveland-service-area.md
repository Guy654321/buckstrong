# Cleveland service area and location-page selection

## Selection method

This is a practical Cleveland dispatch territory, not every point inside a circle. The 55 additions below, combined with the 15 existing Cleveland records, make **70 location pages**. All new place/township centers are within 29 straight-line miles of downtown Cleveland; driving distance and appointment availability still vary. The outermost pages are Amherst, Painesville, Medina, Chardon, Streetsboro, Lorain, Aurora, and Mentor. The website asks customers to confirm service at their address. Akron, Cuyahoga Falls, Kent, Vermilion, Madison, Wadsworth, and other farther corridors are excluded.

Candidates were screened for distinct municipal or recognized neighborhood identity, 2024 population/household scale where available, residential garage relevance, realistic route grouping, and enough specific context for a useful page. Census-derived populations and straight-line center distances are **selection aids**, not claims about exact service coverage. Owner occupancy and detached-home patterns were considered with Census QuickFacts and municipal housing context; no unsupported city-level rate is put into page copy. A small municipality may still qualify when it has a distinct residential search area.

Postal ZIPs below are representative booking-area identifiers, not municipal boundaries or a guarantee that every address in a ZIP is covered. Several ZIPs span neighboring jurisdictions. Neighborhood pages describe portions of Cleveland and share Cleveland postal ZIPs; they are not separate cities.

## Editorial and operational boundaries

- Cleveland neighborhood pages are limited to West Park, Old Brooklyn, Lee-Harvard, and North Collinwood, which the city recognizes as residential areas. Other neighborhoods remain covered by the Cleveland hub instead of receiving near-duplicate pages.
- Very small villages and townships overlapping these selected postal/search areas stay on neighboring pages. East Cleveland, Downtown, University Circle, and denser entertainment/commercial neighborhoods were not selected as garage-focused residential pages.
- Amherst, Lorain, Painesville, Chardon, Medina, Streetsboro, and Aurora are outer-edge pages. Dispatch should confirm capacity by street address before launch. No page promises a response time or an office in that community.
- Cleveland bookings use the owner-supplied (216) 930-2980 line. The owner-supplied address at 24500 Center Ridge Rd, Ste. 275-1, Westlake, OH 44145 is published as the Cleveland-market address. The site does not describe it as a staffed walk-in office. The Cleveland booking line is presented as open 24/7; appointment availability still depends on address and scheduling.
