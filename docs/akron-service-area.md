# Akron service-area selection

Research date: 23 October 2026. Selection is a practical dispatch and content plan, subject to owner confirmation of appointment capacity. Census estimates screen residential scale, owner occupancy, and single-unit structures; they do not prove an individual home has a garage. Single-unit includes attached and detached houses. Figures are ACS 2020–2024 five-year estimates, not 2026 counts. Margins of error are retained in the raw source snapshot; percentages are rounded and used as screening aids.

## Selected surrounding communities

Municipalities and townships are distinct geographies. ZIPs are representative postal identifiers, not exclusive jurisdiction/service boundaries or a promise to serve every address. No selected Akron ZIP appears in the existing Cleveland hub ZIP list. Center miles are straight-line Census internal-point distances from Akron, not driving distances.

The adjacent Summit County communities form the primary coverage area. Bath, Silver Lake, Norton, New Franklin, Springfield, Mogadore, and Suffield have strong owner-occupied or single-unit housing indicators. Cuyahoga Falls, Stow, Tallmadge, Green, Wadsworth, and Jackson Township add substantial residential scale. Kent and Ravenna have lower ownership rates but enough distinct residential housing and business demand to justify a page; do not describe them as uniformly owner-occupied suburbs. Smaller villages are retained for their distinct identities and adjacent route practicality, rather than treated as large cities.

## Akron neighborhoods

One hub covers Akron generally. Six recognized residential neighborhoods receive their own pages: Ellet (44312), Goodyear Heights (44305), Firestone Park (44301), Kenmore (44314), Northwest Akron (44313), and Wallhaven (44313). Their `Place` schema is contained in Akron; they are not separate municipalities. Use the City of Akron's 2023 neighborhood boundaries and GIS labels. No separate neighborhood ACS figures are asserted. Downtown, University Park, and other small/overlapping neighborhoods remain on the hub. Do not create a second Fairlawn page under the Akron neighborhood name Fairlawn Heights.

## Cleveland boundary and deferred pages

The existing Cleveland records are the authority for market ownership. No Akron page is created for Hudson, Twinsburg, Macedonia, Sagamore Hills, Northfield Center, Medina, Brunswick, Aurora, Streetsboro, Brecksville, or any other existing Cleveland locality. Richfield, Peninsula, and Boston Heights are reserved transition areas. There is no claim that routing territories follow whole ZIP boundaries. Shared postal names inside Akron's territory (Portage Lakes, Manchester, Montrose, Greentown) are handled within relevant selected locations without duplicate landing pages. Canton, Massillon, Wooster, Alliance, and more distant rural markets are deferred.
